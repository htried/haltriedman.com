I'm a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at Cornell Tech, working with Vitaly Shmatikov on privacy, security, and AI. I'm an NSF Graduate Research Fellow, and was previously supported by a Cornell University Fellowship.

From February to August 2026, I was a Student Researcher at Google, working on LLM post-training to make agents safer and less prone to meltdowns.

From 2021 to 2024, I was a Senior Privacy Engineer at the Wikimedia Foundation, working on differential privacy, algorithmic accountability and transparency, and global accessibility. I'm still working with Wikimedia Enterprise on using Wikimedia's public data for quick and accurate geolocated trend analysis.

I also sometimes write and edit for Reboot and Kernel Magazine.

I did a B.S. in Computer Science and a B.A. in History at Brown University, graduating in December 2020.

I played keys and wrote music with Birdnest from 2021 to 2024, and was a founding member of Honeycomb before that. My new project, Trouble Department, is coming soon.
